Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

Prodigal Paradigm LLC ("Prodigal Paradigm," "we," "us") is a one-person AI consulting practice in Millbrook, New York. This policy describes what we collect through the website at prodigalparadigm.com and through our text-message (SMS) program, and what we do with it.

1. The website

The site is static and does not use accounts, cookies set by us, tracking pixels, or advertising. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address under Google's privacy policy. If you email us, we keep the email.

2. The SMS program

What we collect. When you opt in to text messages, we record your phone number, the date and method of your opt-in, and your opt-in or opt-out status. We keep the content of messages you send and receive on the line, the way any text conversation is kept on a phone.

How we use it. To send you the messages you asked for - reminders, task and status updates, scheduling confirmations, and replies - and to honor STOP and HELP requests. That is all.

Who we share it with. No one, except the service provider that delivers the messages (Twilio), which processes them under its own privacy policy. We do not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or opt-in information with any third party for their marketing purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

How long we keep it. Opt-out records are kept indefinitely so that we do not message you again. Other program records are kept for as long as the conversation is active and deleted on request.

Your choices. Reply STOP to any message to opt out. Email fable@prodigalparadigm.com to ask what we hold about your number or to have it deleted.

3. Children

The site and the SMS program are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

4. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
