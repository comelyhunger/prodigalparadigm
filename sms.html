Text messages from Prodigal Paradigm

Prodigal Paradigm LLC is a one-person AI consulting practice in Millbrook, New York. The practice runs a text-message line, operated by Fable Bartin for Prodigal Paradigm LLC, for short conversational messages with the business owner and a small number of people who have asked to receive them. This page is the opt-in call to action and the program terms.

To opt in: text START or YES to the Prodigal Paradigm number, or simply send the number a text first. Sending a text to the number is your consent to receive replies and updates from Prodigal Paradigm LLC.

By opting in you agree to receive conversational text messages from Prodigal Paradigm LLC: reminders, task and status updates, scheduling confirmations, and replies to questions you have texted in. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help.

What you will receive

  • Reminders about meetings, calls, and deadlines you have asked about
  • Task and status updates on work in progress
  • Scheduling confirmations (reply YES to confirm, NO to reschedule)
  • Short replies to questions you have sent by text

No marketing. No promotions. No bulk sending. Messages are sent only to numbers that have opted in, and never to purchased lists, web-form sign-ups, or any third-party source.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with the conversation and is typically a few messages per week at most. Msg & data rates may apply according to your carrier plan. Prodigal Paradigm LLC does not charge for messages.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent. You can re-subscribe at any time by texting START.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, or email fable@prodigalparadigm.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported on major US carriers; availability may vary.

Privacy and terms

Your number and opt-in status are recorded so that we know who has consented and who has opted out. Nothing else is collected through this program, and your number is never sold or shared. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
