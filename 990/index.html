the region's nonprofit money, from the public record

Every tax-exempt organization in Dutchess, Ulster, Columbia, and Greene counties - the foundations that give, the charities that do, and the money each one reports - straight from the IRS public record. Built for the region's nonprofits: find your neighbors, find your funders, see what the record already says. Free, no signup, nothing kept. Every name links to its full filings.

Source: IRS Exempt Organizations Business Master File, New York extract. Money figures are book values from each organization's latest processed return - the "as of" column says which year. Organizations are placed by mailing address, so a PO box in another town can occasionally misplace one.

How to read it. "Grantmaking foundations" are the private foundations that exist to give money away - the pots. "Operating foundations" run their own programs (gardens, museums, institutes) and rarely make outside grants. Assets and revenue come from the most recent return the IRS has processed; a blank or zero usually means the organization files the 990-N postcard and reports no figures.

Where it comes from. The IRS Business Master File is the public register of every tax-exempt organization in the country. This page carries a monthly snapshot of it, filtered to the four counties, with each organization linked to its full filing history at ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer. Nothing here is private, inferred, or scraped - it is the public record, made readable.

What this page does not do. It sends nothing while you use it: filters, search, and the CSV download all run in your browser. Known gap in this snapshot: organizations filing from Kingston PO boxes (ZIP 12402).
